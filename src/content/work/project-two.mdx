## Design

### What we drew

We rode along for two shifts before drawing anything. The paper was not the
problem — the problem was that confirmation happened once, at the end, from
memory. We moved it to the doorstep and made it a single gesture.

## Build

### What we shipped

Offline-first, because the depot basement and two underpasses on every route
have no signal. Actions queue locally and reconcile on reconnect; the driver
never sees a spinner they cannot escape.

<div class="placeholder" style="height: 420px">Sync architecture — queue, conflict handling, event log</div>

## Outcome

Six hours of end-of-shift reconciliation disappeared, and door-step confirmation
went from 40% to 91%. Dispatch now sees a failed delivery within the hour rather
than the next morning.

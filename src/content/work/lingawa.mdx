## Design

### What we drew

Seven ways to learn the same language (live tutoring, self-paced lessons,
conversation practice, and more) that had to feel like one path rather than
seven products sharing a login. The design work was mostly about making
progress legible across all of them.

### The system, built in flight

The engineering team was small and needed to build immediately, so the system
grew screen by screen: design one, extract what repeats, componentise it, move
on. After three screens there were buttons, inputs and a colour system. After
ten, a full library feeding every new design.

The cost of that approach showed up in the earliest screens, which carried
spacing decisions made before a scale existed. We went back and normalised them
once the library had settled.

## Outcome

Lingawa serves over 6,000 learners across the diaspora and beyond, holds 5.0
stars on both app stores and 4.9 on Trustpilot, and raised $1.1M.

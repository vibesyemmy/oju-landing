## Design

### The problem with design feedback

Every designer knows the cycle: finish a screen, wait for someone to look at
it, receive feedback that is mostly mechanical. Spacing, wrong component,
contrast failure, missing loading state. None of it required a person. It
required a checklist with eyes.

### Six critics, each with a point of view

The mistake most design checkers make is generic feedback. Each critic here has
a distinct domain and voice: an accessibility auditor covering contrast, touch
targets, focus order and screen reader labels; a visual design critic on colour
harmony, type scale, the 8px grid and whitespace; a usability tester; and three
more.

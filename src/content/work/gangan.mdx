## Design

### The insight

Both groups already had the relationship. Artists build audiences on stage, on
social media, in their local scene. The product did not need to build discovery
or algorithmic recommendation. It needed to be a direct payment rail between
people who already wanted to support each other.

That single decision removed an entire product surface and shaped everything
after it.

### The identity

A talking drum, reduced to a silhouette. Streaming services are green, red,
black. Gangan needed to signal different economics at a glance, before anyone
read the pitch. The mark carries across the App Store and Play Store listings,
the marketing site, social, and inside the product as app icon, loading screen
and navigation mark.

## Outcome

Gangan launched across web, iOS and Android from a single design system serving
two distinct personas, with a cohesive identity across all platforms and
marketing surfaces.
